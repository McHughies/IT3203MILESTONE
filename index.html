OVERVIEW

Abstract & research focus

Web application security is the practice of protecting websites and their users from unauthorized access, data exposure, and misuse. This introductory research explores representative threats, including injection and broken access control, before examining practical defensive techniques. The goal is to translate technical risks into clear recommendations for students and beginning developers.
